Frequently asked questions

我的日记会上传到云端吗？ Will my journal be uploaded to the cloud? ▼
不会。所有日记、照片、AI 内容都保存在你的 iPhone 本地数据库（SwiftData）中，从不发送到任何服务器。Via拾光 没有后端，没有账号系统。 No. All journals, photos, and AI content are saved in your iPhone's local database (SwiftData) and never sent to any server. Via Journal has no backend and no account system.
AI 功能需要网络吗？ Does the AI feature require an internet connection? ▼
不需要。Via拾光 使用 Apple Foundation Models——一种完全运行在设备端的大语言模型，无需联网。若设备暂不支持，Via拾光 会使用内置模板生成内容，同样不需要网络。 No. Via Journal uses Apple Foundation Models — a large language model running entirely on your device with no internet required. If your device doesn't support it yet, Via Journal uses built-in templates to generate content, also with no network needed.
如何备份和恢复数据？ How do I back up and restore my data? ▼
前往「设置 → 导出数据」，选择「完整备份（JSON）」。这份文件包含你所有的日记、总结、照片和 AI 内容。换机或重装 App 后，在同一位置选择「导入备份」即可恢复。 Go to Settings → Export Data and choose "Full Backup (JSON)." This file contains all your journals, summaries, photos, and AI content. After switching devices or reinstalling, tap "Import Backup" in the same location to restore everything.
如何删除所有数据？ How do I delete all my data? ▼
前往「设置 → 删除所有数据」，输入"DELETE"确认后，所有日记、总结和 AI 内容将被彻底清除。此操作不可撤销，建议先导出完整备份。卸载 App 也会从设备上删除所有数据。 Go to Settings → Delete All Data, type "DELETE" to confirm, and all journals, summaries, and AI content will be permanently erased. This cannot be undone — we recommend exporting a full backup first. Uninstalling the app also removes all data from your device.
什么时候生成周 / 月 / 季度总结？ When are weekly / monthly / quarterly summaries generated? ▼
每当你完成某个周期最后一天的记录，或者下次打开 App 时，Via拾光 会自动检查并生成该周期的总结。首份总结在你累积满 7 天记录后生成。 Summaries are generated automatically when you complete the last day's entry of a period, or the next time you open the app. Your first summary is generated after you've recorded 7 days.
忘记记录了怎么办？可以补记吗？ I forgot to journal. Can I add a past entry? ▼
可以。Via拾光 支持「待补全」功能。在日历或历史时间线中，你可以直接点击任何未记录的日期进行补写，或者通过历史顶部的提醒横幅一键补记。当然，跳过某些天完全没问题，Via拾光 没有任何连续打卡压力，一切以你的节奏为准。 Yes. Via Journal supports backdated entries. You can tap any empty date on the calendar tab or timeline to add an entry, or use the retroactive banner in the history tab. Skipping a day is still completely fine — Via Journal won't pressure you.
Via拾光 支持哪些 iPhone 型号？ Which iPhone models does Via Journal support? ▼
Via拾光 需要 iOS 17.0 或更新版本。Apple Foundation Models 的 AI 功能需要支持该特性的设备（目前为搭载 A17 Pro 及以上芯片或 M 系列芯片的设备）；其他设备可使用模板生成版本的 AI 内容，所有其他功能完全可用。 Via Journal requires iOS 17.0 or later. The AI features powered by Apple Foundation Models require a supported device (currently A17 Pro or later, or M-series chip). On other devices, Via Journal uses template-generated AI content — all other features work fully.
如何切换中文 / 英文？ How do I switch between Chinese and English? ▼
Via拾光 会自动跟随 iOS 系统语言（中文系统 → 中文，其他语言 → 英文）。你也可以前往「设置 → 语言」手动切换，切换后 AI 生成的内容也会随之改变语言。 Via Journal automatically follows your iOS system language (Chinese system → Chinese; other languages → English). You can also go to Settings → Language to switch manually. AI-generated content will follow your chosen language.
