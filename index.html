设计理念

做少，但做得深

Via 不是一款要你打卡、攒成就的 App。它只是一个安静的地方，让你每天停下来一会儿，写下真实的自己。

Philosophy

Do less. Feel more.

Via isn't an app that rewards streaks or gamifies your habits. It's a quiet place to pause each day and write something true about yourself.

🔒

私密第一

所有数据存在你的设备上。没有服务器，没有账号，没有任何人能看到你写的内容。

Privacy first

All data lives on your device. No servers, no accounts, no one else can ever read what you write.

🌱

低负担

4 个问题，几分钟完成。没有强制连续打卡，漏了也没关系，随时可以回来。

Low friction

Four prompts, a few minutes. No mandatory streaks — missed a day? That's fine. Come back whenever.

🤖

AI 在本地

使用 Apple 设备端大模型分析你的记录，日记内容不会离开你的手机半步。

AI on your device

Powered by Apple's on-device Foundation Models — your journal never leaves your phone to reach any AI server.

🌊

长期陪伴

周、月、季度、年度复盘帮你看见自己的成长轨迹，而不是孤立的某一天。

Long-term perspective

Weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual reflections help you see your growth over time — not just isolated days.

使用流程

三步开始，终生受益

Via 的流程极度简单。你只需要每天花几分钟，剩下的交给时间。

How it works

Start in minutes. Benefit for years.

Via's flow is deliberately simple. Spend a few minutes each day — and let time do the rest.

1

每天回答 4 个问题

今天最重要的产出是什么？什么浪费了你的时间？明天最想做什么？还有一段自由日记。支持富文本、照片和语音输入。

Answer 4 daily prompts

What was your most important output? What drained your time? What do you most want to do tomorrow? Plus a free-form journal. Supports rich text, photos, and voice input.

2

AI 提炼你的生活线索

完成当天记录后，Via 的 AI 会给你一条温和的日反馈。首页还会实时显示最近 7 条记录中提炼出的产出方向、时间模式和下一步意图。

AI distills your life threads

After each entry, Via's AI gives you a gentle daily reflection. Your home screen also shows live patterns from your last 7 entries — output direction, time habits, next intentions.

3

长期总结，看见自己

记录满 7 天后，Via 生成你的第一份周观察报告。之后按月、季度、年度持续复盘，每份总结都有 AI 提炼的暖心标题，像一位私人教练陪你回望。

Periodic reflections reveal patterns

After 7 days, Via generates your first Weekly Observation. Monthly, quarterly, and annual summaries follow — each with an AI-crafted title that feels like a gentle coach reflecting back what you've done.

Support

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Email us — we usually reply within 72 hours.

我的日记会上传到云端吗？ Will my journal be uploaded to the cloud? ▼
不会。所有日记、照片、AI 内容都保存在你的 iPhone 本地数据库（SwiftData）中，从不发送到任何服务器。Via 没有后端，没有账号系统。 No. All journals, photos, and AI content are saved in your iPhone's local database (SwiftData) and never sent to any server. Via has no backend and no account system.
AI 功能需要网络吗？ Does the AI feature require an internet connection? ▼
不需要。Via 使用 Apple Foundation Models——一种完全运行在设备端的大语言模型，无需联网。若设备暂不支持，Via 会使用内置模板生成内容，同样不需要网络。 No. Via uses Apple Foundation Models — a large language model running entirely on your device with no internet required. If your device doesn't support it yet, Via uses built-in templates to generate content, also with no network needed.
如何备份和恢复数据？ How do I back up and restore my data? ▼
前往「设置 → 导出数据」，选择「完整备份（JSON）」。这份文件包含你所有的日记、总结、照片和 AI 内容。换机或重装 App 后，在同一位置选择「导入备份」即可恢复。 Go to Settings → Export Data and choose "Full Backup (JSON)." This file contains all your journals, summaries, photos, and AI content. After switching devices or reinstalling, tap "Import Backup" in the same location to restore everything.
如何删除所有数据？ How do I delete all my data? ▼
前往「设置 → 删除所有数据」，输入"DELETE"确认后，所有日记、总结和 AI 内容将被彻底清除。此操作不可撤销，建议先导出完整备份。卸载 App 也会从设备上删除所有数据。 Go to Settings → Delete All Data, type "DELETE" to confirm, and all journals, summaries, and AI content will be permanently erased. This cannot be undone — we recommend exporting a full backup first. Uninstalling the app also removes all data from your device.
什么时候生成周 / 月 / 季度总结？ When are weekly / monthly / quarterly summaries generated? ▼
每当你完成某个周期最后一天的记录，或者下次打开 App 时，Via 会自动检查并生成该周期的总结。首份总结在你累积满 7 天记录后生成。 Summaries are generated automatically when you complete the last day's entry of a period, or the next time you open the app. Your first summary is generated after you've recorded 7 days.
忘记记录了怎么办？可以补记吗？ I forgot to journal. Can I add a past entry? ▼
Via 目前专注于当日记录，暂不支持补记过去的日期。但是跳过某天完全没问题，Via 不会催你，随时回来都可以继续。 Via currently focuses on today's entry and doesn't support backdated entries. But skipping a day is completely fine — Via won't nag you. Come back whenever you're ready.
Via 支持哪些 iPhone 型号？ Which iPhone models does Via support? ▼
Via 需要 iOS 17.0 或更新版本。Apple Foundation Models 的 AI 功能需要支持该特性的设备（目前为搭载 A17 Pro 及以上芯片或 M 系列芯片的设备）；其他设备可使用模板生成版本的 AI 内容，所有其他功能完全可用。 Via requires iOS 17.0 or later. The AI features powered by Apple Foundation Models require a supported device (currently A17 Pro or later, or M-series chip). On other devices, Via uses template-generated AI content — all other features work fully.
如何切换中文 / 英文？ How do I switch between Chinese and English? ▼
Via 会自动跟随 iOS 系统语言（中文系统 → 中文，其他语言 → 英文）。你也可以前往「设置 → 语言」手动切换，切换后 AI 生成的内容也会随之改变语言。 Via automatically follows your iOS system language (Chinese system → Chinese; other languages → English). You can also go to Settings → Language to switch manually. AI-generated content will follow your chosen language.
